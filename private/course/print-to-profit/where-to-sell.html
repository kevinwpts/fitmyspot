The short answer

Etsy isn't the only place, and it isn't always the best one. Start with two channels: one online marketplace where your buyers already search, plus one local or personal channel for your first sales. In this chapter you'll see what each place costs, what sells there, and how to pick yours in 10 minutes.

The rule: go where your buyers already search

Don't pick a channel because a YouTuber uses it. Pick it because your buyer already shops there. Someone looking for a broken dishwasher part searches eBay or Google. Someone looking for a name sign for a kid's room searches Etsy or Pinterest. Someone in your town looking for a custom fix asks in a local Facebook group.

The Etsy fee most sellers miss

Offsite Ads. When Etsy advertises your item on Google or social media and that ad leads to a sale, Etsy takes an extra 15% (12% for shops over $10,000 a year, max $100 per order). Shops under $10,000 a year can switch it off under Settings → Offsite Ads. Bigger shops can't. Either way, price as if some orders will have it.

Fees change

These are typical US numbers from late 2026. They differ by country and change often. Check the fee page of each platform before you set your price (chapter 6).

Which channel fits your product?

Personalized items

Etsy + local. Buyers search "custom", "personalized", "name" there.

Your own designs

Etsy first, TikTok Shop when you make videos.

Replacement parts

eBay + Google. Put the exact part or model number in the title.

Custom jobs nearby

Facebook Marketplace and local groups. No fees, no shipping.

Business customers

Direct messages and visits. Skip marketplaces.

Seasonal and gifts

Etsy + markets and fairs in November and December.

Pick your channels in 10 minutes

01

Search your product on 3 platforms

Search the same 2–3 words on Etsy, eBay and Facebook Marketplace. Where do you find similar items with reviews or sales? That's where buyers are.

02

Pick one main marketplace

The one with the most proof from step 1. Only one for now. Every extra platform means more listings, messages and fees to keep track of.

03

Pick one local or personal channel

Your local Facebook group, a neighbourhood app or your country's second-hand site. For example: Blocket (Sweden), Kleinanzeigen (Germany), Leboncoin (France), Marktplaats (Netherlands), Gumtree (UK, Australia) or Kijiji (Canada). This is where your first sales and first reviews usually come from.

04

Put the right fees in your price

Each channel takes a different cut. Put your main channel's fees in the pricing worksheet in the next chapter. If you sell in two places, use the higher fee, so every sale is worth it.

Selling from Instagram or TikTok without a shop

You don't need a website to sell to your followers. Use a payment link: Stripe and PayPal both let you create a link for one product in a few minutes. Put it in your bio, or send it in a DM:

Thanks for asking! It's $[price] including shipping to [country]. Pay here and I'll ship within [X] days: [link]. Want a name on it? Just reply with the name.

Make a payment link with Stripe in 5 minutes

01

Create a free Stripe account

Go to stripe.com, sign up and verify your identity and bank account.

02

Add your product

Product catalog → Add product. Name, photo and price. For a physical product, also turn on shipping address collection when you create the link.

03

Create the link

Payment Links → New. Pick the product. Under options, tick Collect customers' addresses and choose the countries you ship to. Tap Create link and copy it.

04

Share it

Paste it in your Instagram bio, your link page, or DMs. You get an email for every order, with the buyer's address.

PayPal works the same way: PayPal Business → Pay Links and Buttons.

Kevin's tip

Make videos even if you sell on Etsy

A short video can send buyers to your Etsy listing. Those visits and sales also help the listing in Etsy search. Your videos and your shop work together.

What to save for later

Good later

  • Your own website: great once people already know you
  • Wholesale: great once a product sells steadily

Not now

  • 5 platforms at once: you spread too thin and no listing gets traction
  • Amazon Handmade: you must apply and show handwork. Pure 3D prints can be rejected
