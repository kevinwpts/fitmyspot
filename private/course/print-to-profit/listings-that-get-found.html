The short answer

A good listing does two jobs: gets found (the words buyers search for) and gets bought (clear benefits, size, and no doubts left). Use the templates in this chapter: title, description, tags and FAQ. Copy, fill in, done.

How buyers find products

Buyers don't search for "3D printed holder". They search for what they need:

  • "switch game holder"
  • "ikea alex drawer organizer"
  • "personalized cat name sign"

Your job is to use their words, not yours.

Write your listing in 5 steps

01

Find the words buyers use

Start typing your product into Etsy, eBay or Google search and don't press enter. The suggestions that pop up are real searches. Write down 10–15 of them.

Also note the words used in the titles of the best-selling similar listings.

02

Write the title

Put the most important words first. Template:

[What it is] for [what it fits/who it's for], [key feature], [option]

Examples:

  • ✅ "Switch Game Holder for 24 Cartridges, Desk Organizer, Multiple Colors"
  • ✅ "Personalized Cat Name Sign for Door, Custom Pet Plaque"
  • ❌ "Cool 3D Printed Thing ⭐ Best Gift ⭐"

Keep it readable. A human has to want to click it.

03

Write the description

Most buyers only read the first lines. Put the answers they need at the top. Template:

[One line: what it does for them.] Keeps your 24 Switch games tidy and easy to find.

What you get: [item, size in cm and inches, color] Fits: [exact models / sizes] Made by me: Designed and 3D printed by me in [city], in [material]. Personalization: [how to add a name / options] Shipping: Ships in [X] days, [carrier]. Care: [e.g. keep away from heat, like a hot car]

Short sentences. Benefits first, specs after.

04

Add tags and attributes

Use all the tags the marketplace allows. Fill them with the search phrases you collected, not single words:

  • "switch game storage" instead of "storage"
  • "gift for gamer" instead of "gift"

Fill in every attribute too (color, material, occasion). Marketplaces use them in search filters.

05

Answer doubts before they're asked

Add a short FAQ at the end of the description. Every question a buyer has is a reason to leave. Common ones:

  • Will it fit my [thing]? → exact measurements
  • Is it sturdy? → material, wall thickness, what it can hold
  • Can I choose the color? → yes/no and options
  • How long until it arrives? → making time + shipping

Make every listing look like a brand

Same photo style

Same background and light on every listing.

Same title pattern

What + for whom + key feature.

A short shop story

"I design and print everything myself in [city]." Buyers love a real person.

Ask for reviews

A friendly note in the package. Reviews are the best sales tool there is.

Your own shop or Instagram?

The same rules apply. Write what it does, show the size, make buying easy. On Instagram, put the price and how to buy in the caption, and the link in your bio.

Don't stuff keywords

Titles like "Holder Organizer Stand Storage Box Gift Desk Gamer Switch" look spammy and can hurt you. Write for humans, using buyers' words.

Etsy's newest rules for titles (2026)

Etsy updated its title guidance in April 2026. In short:

Do

  • Keep titles short: under about 15 words
  • Name the item clearly, most important traits first (what it is, color, material, size)
  • Put extra details in tags, attributes and the description

Don't

  • Subjective words like "perfect" or "beautiful"
  • Gift phrases and holidays, unless essential to the item
  • Repeated words, sale or shipping info

Etsy now looks at the whole listing: title, tags, attributes, description, the first photo and even reviews. The title doesn't have to carry every keyword anymore.

Before: "Switch Game Holder Organizer Storage Box Gift for Gamer Perfect Christmas Present Desk Stand" After: "Switch Game Holder for 24 Cartridges, Black PLA, Desk Organizer"

The tag formula

Etsy gives you 13 tags (each up to 20 characters). Use all 13, as multi-word phrases. Spread them like this:

Tags What they describe Example
4 What it is switch game holder, cartridge storage
3 What it fits / who it's for nintendo switch, gamer gift
2 Where it's used desk organizer, game room decor
2 Style or material minimalist, 3d printed
2 Occasions (only if real) birthday gift, christmas gift

Phrases you already selected as attributes (like color or occasion) don't need a tag too. Use that space for another search phrase.

4 things most sellers miss

Search visibility page

In Shop Manager, Etsy shows a score for your shop and listings, with tips. Check it monthly.

Reply fast

Etsy's search looks at your review rating, message response rate and cases over the past 3 months.

Use the policy template

Etsy says filling in your shop policies with their template gives a boost.

Refresh before seasons

Update titles and tags 6–8 weeks before big holidays.
