Quick answer

Your first 10 sales almost never come from search. They come from people you can reach directly: your network, local groups, hobby communities and short videos. Do one action from this chapter every day for 2 weeks, and track what works.

Why search won't bring your first sales

New listings have no reviews and no sales history, so marketplaces show them less. That's normal. Your first sales give you the reviews that make search work later. So for now, you bring the buyers.

6 ways to get your first buyers (no ads)

1

1. Your own network

Tell people you know. Not "please buy", but "look what I made":

  • Post it on your personal Instagram or Facebook
  • Send it to 5 friends who fit the buyer type
  • Ask: "Do you know anyone who'd like this?"

Your first 1–3 sales often come from one step away.

2

2. Local groups and marketplaces

Local Facebook groups, neighbourhood apps and second-hand marketplaces. People love buying from someone nearby.

Post the hero photo, the price and one line: "I design and 3D print these myself in [town]. Pick-up or shipping."

3

3. Hobby communities

Go where your buyers hang out: Reddit, Discord servers, Facebook groups, forums for your hobby.

Give before you sell

Most communities don't allow pure ads. Be a member first: answer questions, share how you made it, show the problem it solves. Only link to your shop where the rules allow it.

4

4. Short videos

A 10–20 second vertical video beats any photo:

  • The problem (messy drawer) → the product → the result (tidy)
  • The print growing on the plate, timelapse
  • "I made this because…" in your own voice

Post on Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts. Same video, three places. End with: "Link in bio."

5

5. Local shops and cafés

Small shops, gift shops and cafés sometimes sell local products on commission. Bring a sample, keep it short: "I make these locally. Would you like to sell a few? You keep [X]%."

6

6. Markets and fairs

Christmas markets, craft fairs, maker fairs. Great for personalized items: people order on the spot. You also learn fast what people pick up and what they ignore.

Your 14-day sales sprint

One small action per day. Check it off.

Day Action
1 Post your product on your personal account
2 Send it to 5 people who fit the buyer type
3 Post in 1 local group
4 Join 2 hobby communities, help someone
5 Film and post a 15-second video
6 Ask a happy buyer (or friend) for a review
7 Look at your numbers: views, clicks, messages
8 Post a second video with a different angle
9 Share in a hobby community (where allowed)
10 Contact 1 local shop or café
11 Improve your weakest photo or your title
12 Post a third video: the making-of
13 Offer a small launch deal to your network
14 Review: what brought sales? Do more of that.

Track what works

A simple note per sale: where did this buyer come from?

Count everything

Views, messages, sales, per channel.

Double down

The channel that brought sales gets more of your time.

Drop the rest

No result after 2 weeks? Stop doing it.

Turn buyers into fans

Ask for reviews

Friendly note in the package + a message after delivery.

Ask for photos

"Would you send me a photo of it in use?" Then share it (with permission).

Reply fast

Answer every message within a day.

Remember them

Repeat buyers are the cheapest buyers.

Your first review is gold

Ask your first few buyers personally for a review. One honest review makes the next sale much easier.

  • Film your first 15-second video
