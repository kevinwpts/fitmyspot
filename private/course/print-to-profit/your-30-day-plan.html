The short answer

This is the whole guide as a day-by-day plan. About 30–60 minutes a day. Each day links back to the chapter that explains it. Don't skip ahead, and don't stop to "perfect" anything. Done is better than perfect.

Kevin's tip

Missed a day?

Just continue the next day. The plan works if you finish it, not if you follow it perfectly.

After day 30

10+ sales?

Go to chapter 11 and start saving time and raising value per order.

A few sales?

Keep the 14-day sprint going. Double down on the channel that worked.

No sales?

Normal for some. Check: was there proof of demand? Are the photos clear? Is the price right? Then try idea #2 from your top 3.

The only real failure

The only way this doesn't work is stopping. Most sellers who make money had a first product that flopped. They just kept going.

Your day-30 review

Answer these honestly:

  1. Which product got the most interest?
  2. Which channel brought the most buyers?
  3. What took the most time?
  4. What would you do differently?
  5. What's your goal for the next 30 days?
