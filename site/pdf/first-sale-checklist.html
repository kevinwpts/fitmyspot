The First Sale Checklist

12 steps from "I have a 3D printer" to "someone paid me". Print it, tick the boxes, do one step a day.

1Pick
  • Choose one type of buyer"Board gamers", "cat owners", "people with small flats". Not "everyone".
  • Write down 3 problems they haveRead reviews, forums and Reddit: look for "I wish…" and "doesn't fit…".
  • Pick one product idea that solves one of themSimple beats clever. Organizers, holders, personalized items.
2Check
  • Make sure you're allowed to sell itYour own design = safe. Someone else's: read the license. "NC" or "Standard Digital File License" = no selling.
  • No characters, logos or brand namesWhatever the license says.
  • Selling on Etsy? It must be your own designOr personalized for the buyer. Etsy's rules since 2025.
3Make
  • Design or adapt it, then print a testUse it for real for a few days. Fix what annoys you.
  • Work out your priceFilament + power + machine + your time + packaging, × 2–3, + fees. boredprinter.com/tools/3d-print-price-calculator
  • Take 5 photos in daylightClean background, one in use, one showing size (hand or ruler).
4Sell
  • Post it where your buyers already areA local group, marketplace, Etsy, a hobby forum, or your own Instagram.
  • Write what it does, not what it is"Keeps 8 Switch cartridges tidy" beats "3D printed holder".
  • Ask 5 people you know to share itYour first sale often comes from one step away.
