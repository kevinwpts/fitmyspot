3D print ideas for your car

Cup holders, vents, door pockets and seat gaps are full of small problems a 3D print can fix. Use PETG or ASA, since PLA can warp in a hot car.

  1. 1. Cup holder organizer

    Turns an empty cup holder into a tray for keys, coins and cards.

    Material: PETG

  2. 2. Vent phone mount

    Keeps your phone in view for maps instead of sliding on the seat.

    Material: PETG

  3. 3. Seat gap filler

    Stops phones and keys from falling between the seat and console.

    Material: TPU

  4. 4. Sunglasses clip for the visor

    Keeps sunglasses safe and easy to grab.

    Material: PETG

  5. 5. Trash bin for the door pocket

    A small bin that fits the door pocket, so wrappers stay in one place.

    Material: PETG

  6. 6. Cable clips for the dashboard

    Keeps the charging cable from falling into the footwell.

    Material: PETG

  7. 7. Hook for the headrest

    Holds bags so groceries don't roll around.

    Material: PETG

  8. 8. Parking ticket holder

    Holds the ticket or a parking disc on the windshield.

    Material: PETG
