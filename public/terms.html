What FitMySpot does

FitMySpot suggests 3D prints for a photo you take and links to models made by other creators. We don't host or sell any 3D files. Models belong to their creators and follow the license on the site where they are published.

Suggestions

Suggestions are made automatically and can be wrong. Always check the size, material and safety before you print, especially for things used in a car, near heat or by children.

Credits

You get 3 free credits a day. One photo uses one credit. If the analysis fails, the credit is given back. Bought credits don't expire and can't be exchanged for money, except where the law says otherwise.

Fair use

Don't try to get around the limits, overload the service or upload photos you don't have the right to use. We may block accounts that do.
