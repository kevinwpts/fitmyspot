Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Your photos

When you take a photo, it is sent to our AI provider (Google Gemini) to suggest prints. We do not store your photo. It is only used to create your suggestions.

Account

If you sign in, we store your email address, your credits and the text of your saved ideas (never the photo). You can delete your saved ideas any time, and ask us to delete your account.

Usage limits

To give everyone 3 free credits a day and stop abuse, we store a scrambled (hashed) version of your network address together with the date. It can't be used to identify you.

Cookies

We don't use tracking or advertising cookies. If you sign in, your login is saved in your browser so you stay signed in.

Services we use

  • Google Gemini – analyzes photos
  • Supabase (EU) – accounts and credits
  • Netlify – hosting
  • Printables – model images and links

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: use the contact form.
