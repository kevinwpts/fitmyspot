Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Who we are

boredprinter (boredprinter.com) is run by Kevin, a private person in Sweden. Kevin is responsible for your data (the "data controller"). Contact: use the contact form. Messages go straight to Kevin's inbox.

Your photos

When you take a photo, it is sent to Google Gemini to find print ideas. We never store your photo. Google processes it only to create your suggestions.

What we save

  • Account: your email, your password (encrypted, we can't see it), your credits, your invite code and your email settings.
  • My rooms and recent photos: the name of the spot (like "your bathroom"), a short list of things we saw (like "sink, towels") and your print ideas. Text only, never the photo.
  • Thumbs up/down: which ideas you liked, to make suggestions better.
  • Waitlist: if you tap "Notify me", that you want an email when buying credits opens.
  • Abuse protection: a scrambled version of your network address, so the free limits work.
  • On your device only: your printer choice and your login, saved in your browser.

How long we keep it

  • Account, rooms and recent photos: until you delete them or your account.
  • Results when not signed in: 2 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Scrambled network address: 30 days.
  • Thumbs up/down: your name is removed after 90 days.
  • Contact messages: 12 months.

Why we may use it

  • To run the service you signed up for (your account, credits, rooms and ideas).
  • Weekly emails and the "Notify me" email: only if you turn them on. You can turn them off any time, and every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • Abuse protection and the free limits: our legitimate interest in keeping the service working and fair.

Services we use

These companies process data for us. Some are based in the USA. They are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the EU's standard contract clauses.

  • Google Gemini – looks at your photo to find ideas
  • Supabase (servers in the EU) – accounts, credits and My home
  • Resend – sends sign-in, password and weekly emails
  • Vercel – hosting and anonymous visitor stats (no cookies)
  • Brave Search – finds MakerWorld models (only the search words, never your photo)
  • Printables and MakerWorld – we show their model images and link to them

Cookies

We don't use tracking or advertising cookies. Your login is saved in your browser so you stay signed in.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, download or delete your data, and you can object to how we use it. You can delete rooms and your whole account yourself on your account page. For anything else, use the contact form. We answer within 30 days. You can also complain to the Swedish data protection authority, IMY (imy.se).

Age

You must be at least 13 to create an account.

Changes

If we change this policy in a big way, we'll tell you on the site or by email.
