Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Your photos

When you take a photo, it is sent to our AI provider (Google Gemini) to suggest prints. We do not store your photo. It is only used to create your suggestions.

What we save and for how long

  • Signed in: each photo you take becomes a saved spot with its ideas (text only, never the photo). It is kept until you delete it or delete your account. You can delete spots and your account any time from your profile.
  • Not signed in: your ideas are kept for 2 days so you can unlock them by creating an account. Then they are deleted automatically.
  • Usage limits: a scrambled version of your network address is kept for 30 days, then deleted.
  • Contact messages: kept for 12 months.
  • Thumbs up/down: kept without your name after 90 days, to improve suggestions.

Cookies

We don't use tracking or advertising cookies. If you sign in, your login is saved in your browser so you stay signed in.

Services we use

  • Google Gemini – analyzes photos
  • Supabase (EU) – accounts and credits
  • Vercel – hosting and anonymous visitor stats (no cookies)
  • Printables – model images and links

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: use the contact form.
