Questions

Can you really make money with a 3D printer?

Yes, but for most people it's a side income, not a salary. The people who do best sell their own designs to a specific group of buyers and price in their time. That's exactly what Print to Profit teaches.

I'm a complete beginner at selling. Is this for me?

Yes. You need to know how to print. Everything about selling, from picking a product to getting paid, is explained step by step.

What is Print to Profit, exactly?

A complete online guide you read on this site, on your phone or computer. It comes with pricing worksheets, listing templates and a 30-day plan, and you get free updates when rules or fees change.

No. Print to Profit works without it. If you want to design your own products (which Etsy now requires), Design Your Own Bestseller takes you from zero.
