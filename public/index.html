Questions

Are the models free?

Most are. You download them from the creator's page.

What is My home?

Photo each room once. Every week you get new prints that fit what's there.

Do you keep my photo?

No. We only keep a short list of what we saw, like "bed, lamp".

Which printers does it work with?

All of them. Pick yours on your account page and ideas fit its size.

What if an idea doesn't fit?

Bad photo? You get your credit back.

What does it cost?

First photo free, plus 3 credits when you sign up. 1 photo = 1 credit.
