Questions

Are the models free?

Most are. You download them from the creator's page.

Do you keep my photo?

Not unless you're signed in. Then a small copy is kept in Your photos so you can see your ideas again. Delete it any time.

Which printers does it work with?

All of them. Pick yours on your account page and ideas fit its size.

What if an idea doesn't fit?

Tap “+ more” for other models of the same idea. Bad photo? You get your credit back.

What does it cost?

Your first photo is free, no account needed. Sign up and you get 3 more credits. 1 photo = 1 credit.
