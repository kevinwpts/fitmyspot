Since Etsy started requiring original designs, the sellers who can design their own products have a big advantage. The good news: most products that sell are simple shapes. You don't need to be an engineer, you need a method.

## Who it's for

- You print other people's models and want to make your own.
- You've opened a CAD program, got confused and closed it.
- You want products nobody can copy-claim or take down.
