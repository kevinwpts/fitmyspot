> **Rules change.** This guide reflects Etsy's rules and fees as reported up to November 2026. Always check Etsy's own Seller Policy and fee pages before you list.

## What changed in 2025

In June 2025 Etsy updated its **creativity standards**. 3D printed items moved into a category for items made with computerised tools, and the key rule is that they **must be based on the seller's original design**. Personalized or custom items made to a buyer's request are also fine.

In plain words:

- ✅ You designed it yourself (even in Tinkercad) and print it at home.
- ✅ It's personalized for the buyer: their name, their size, their photo.
- ❌ You downloaded someone else's model, even a free or "commercial use" one, and print it.

Etsy didn't make a big announcement about it, and enforcement has been uneven. Don't build your shop on "they probably won't notice". Listings can be removed, and repeated removals can hurt your whole shop.

## What sells well under the new rules

- **Personalized items:** name signs, keychains, pet tags, cake toppers, desk name plates.
- **Organizers made to fit:** drawer inserts, gridfinity-style bins sized for something specific, cable management.
- **Replacement parts you designed** for common household items (no brand logos).
- **Niche accessories** for a hobby you know: board games, aquariums, fishing, cars, music gear.

## Etsy's fees (US, mid-2026)

| Fee | Amount |
|---|---|
| Listing fee | $0.20 per listing, renews every 4 months or when it sells |
| Transaction fee | 6.5% of item price + shipping + gift wrap |
| Payment processing | about 3% + $0.25 per order (US; other countries differ) |
| Offsite Ads | 15% (or 12% for shops over $10k/year) on sales from Etsy's ads |
| Currency conversion | 2.5% if you sell in a different currency than your payout |

For a $20 item with $5 shipping, expect roughly $2.60–$3 in standard fees, before Offsite Ads. Use the [price calculator](/tools/3d-print-price-calculator) and include fees in your price.

## How to keep your listings safe

1. **Design it yourself.** Keep your CAD files and a few screenshots of the design process.
2. **Take real photos** of your own product. Etsy doesn't allow stock photos for these items.
3. **Write that you design and print it yourself** in the description and the About section.
4. **Film a short making-of video.** It's great for buyers and useful proof if a listing is questioned.
5. **Avoid characters, logos and brand names** completely.

## Not on Etsy? Other places to sell

- **Your own shop** (for example a simple site with a payment link).
- **Local marketplaces** and Facebook groups.
- **Markets and fairs**: great for personalized items.
- **eBay and Amazon Handmade** have their own rules; read them before listing.
- **Sell the file instead** on MakerWorld, Printables or Cults.
