::: answer
**Yes, you can still sell 3D prints on Etsy, but only your own designs** (or items personalized for the buyer). Printing someone else's model and listing it isn't allowed since June 2025.
:::

::: note | Rules change
This reflects Etsy's rules and fees as reported up to November 2026. Check Etsy's Seller Policy and fee pages before you list.
:::

## What changed in 2025

Etsy updated its **creativity standards**. 3D printed items must now be **based on the seller's original design**.

::: figure etsy
:::


::: yesno | OK on Etsy | Not OK on Etsy
+ You designed it yourself (even in Tinkercad)
+ It's personalized: their name, size or photo
+ A production partner prints **your** design (listed on your shop)
- Someone else's model, even a free one
- Someone else's model with a "commercial license"
- Stock photos instead of your own
:::

::: warn | Don't count on "they won't notice"
Enforcement has been uneven, but listings can be removed, and repeated removals can hurt your whole shop.
:::

## Set up your listings the safe way

::: step pencil | Design it yourself
Simple shapes are fine. Keep your CAD files and a few screenshots of the design process. They're your proof.
:::

::: step camera | Take your own photos
Real photos of **your** product. Daylight, clean background, one in use, one showing size.
:::

::: step msg | Say that you design and print it
Write it in the description and in your shop's About section: "Designed and printed by me in [your city]."
:::

::: step clock | Film a short making-of
Ten seconds of you designing or printing it. Great for buyers, and proof if a listing is ever questioned.
:::

::: step x | Skip characters and brands
No characters, logos or brand names. Ever.
:::

## What Etsy charges (US, mid-2026)

| Fee | Amount |
|---|---|
| Listing | $0.20 per listing (renews every 4 months or on sale) |
| Transaction | 6.5% of item + shipping |
| Payment processing | about 3% + $0.25 (varies by country) |
| Offsite Ads | 12–15% on sales from Etsy's ads |
| Currency conversion | 2.5% if currencies differ |

**Example:** a $20 item with $5 shipping costs about **$2.60–$3** in normal fees. Put your numbers in the [price calculator](/tools/3d-print-price-calculator).

## What the rule says, word for word

Etsy's policy says items made by a seller (including 3D printing) **"must be produced based on a seller's original design"**. Before June 2025 it also allowed "a templated design or pattern". That part was removed. So changing the name on someone else's template doesn't count as your design.

## Get found: Etsy's 2026 title and tag rules

::: icons
pencil | Short titles | Etsy recommends under about 15 words, most important traits first.
x | No fluff | Skip "perfect", "beautiful", gift phrases and shipping info in the title.
tag | All 13 tags | Multi-word phrases, each up to 20 characters.
truck | Shipping under $6 | For US buyers, Etsy prioritizes listings with shipping under $6.
:::
