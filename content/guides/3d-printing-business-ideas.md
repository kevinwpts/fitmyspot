## How to pick yours

::: figure demand
:::


::: step users | Start with what you know
A hobby, a job, a problem at home. You'll understand the buyers better than anyone.
:::

::: step search | Check for proof
Search for it on Etsy or eBay. Listings with reviews mean people pay for it.
:::

::: step target | Make it specific
Not "organizer", but "insert for the Wingspan box". Specific is easier to sell and to find.
:::

::: step rocket | Test small
Make one version, photograph it in use, post it. Let buyers tell you if it's a winner.
:::

::: do | Do this today
- Pick 3 ideas that match something you know
- Search for each on Etsy and note how many have reviews
- Choose one and sketch your own version
:::
