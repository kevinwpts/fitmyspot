A good listing does two jobs: **gets found** (the words buyers search for) and **gets bought** (clear benefits, size, and no doubts left). Use the templates in this chapter: title, description, tags and FAQ. Copy, fill in, done.
:::

## How buyers find products

Buyers don't search for "3D printed holder". They search for **what they need**:

- "switch game holder"
- "ikea alex drawer organizer"
- "personalized cat name sign"

Your job is to use **their words**, not yours.

## Write your listing in 5 steps

::: step search | Find the words buyers use
Start typing your product into Etsy, eBay or Google search and **don't press enter**. The suggestions that pop up are real searches. Write down 10–15 of them.

Also note the words used in the titles of the best-selling similar listings.
:::

::: step pencil | Write the title
Put the most important words **first**. Template:

**[What it is] for [what it fits/who it's for], [key feature], [option]**

Examples:

- ✅ "Switch Game Holder for 24 Cartridges, Desk Organizer, Multiple Colors"
- ✅ "Personalized Cat Name Sign for Door, Custom Pet Plaque"
- ❌ "Cool 3D Printed Thing ⭐ Best Gift ⭐"

Keep it readable. A human has to want to click it.
:::

::: step list | Write the description
Most buyers only read the first lines. Put the answers they need **at the top**. Template:

> **[One line: what it does for them.]**
> Keeps your 24 Switch games tidy and easy to find.
>
> **What you get:** [item, size in cm and inches, color]
> **Fits:** [exact models / sizes]
> **Made by me:** Designed and 3D printed by me in [city], in [material].
> **Personalization:** [how to add a name / options]
> **Shipping:** Ships in [X] days, [carrier].
> **Care:** [e.g. keep away from heat, like a hot car]

Short sentences. Benefits first, specs after.
:::

::: step tag | Add tags and attributes
Use all the tags the marketplace allows. Fill them with the **search phrases** you collected, not single words:

- "switch game storage" instead of "storage"
- "gift for gamer" instead of "gift"

Fill in every attribute too (color, material, occasion). Marketplaces use them in search filters.
:::

::: step msg | Answer doubts before they're asked
Add a short FAQ at the end of the description. Every question a buyer has is a reason to leave. Common ones:

- **Will it fit my [thing]?** → exact measurements
- **Is it sturdy?** → material, wall thickness, what it can hold
- **Can I choose the color?** → yes/no and options
- **How long until it arrives?** → making time + shipping
:::

## Open your Etsy shop, step by step

It takes about 30 minutes. Have these ready: your ID (passport or driving licence), your bank details and a payment card. Etsy changes its screens now and then, so a button can have a slightly different name.

::: step pointer | Start the shop
Go to **etsy.com/sell** and tap **Open your Etsy shop**. Log in, or create an account with your email.
:::

::: step pencil | Pick language, country and currency
Choose **English** as the shop language if you want to sell worldwide. It can't be changed later. Pick the currency of your bank account, otherwise Etsy charges 2.5% to convert every payout.
:::

::: step tag | Name your shop
4–20 letters or numbers, no spaces. Short, easy to say, and about your niche, for example `DeskFitStudio` or `GameShelfPrints`. Have 2–3 backups ready in case your first choice is taken.
:::

::: step box | Add your first listing
Etsy needs one listing before the shop can open. Use the next section to fill it in, field by field.
:::

::: step coins | Set up how you get paid
Add your bank account. The name must match the account holder exactly. Then verify your identity with your ID (Etsy uses a partner called Persona). Add a card for Etsy's fees, and pay the one-time setup fee if your country has one. Etsy shows the exact amount before you pay.
:::

::: step shield | Turn on two-factor login
**Settings → Security → Two-factor authentication.** It protects your payouts. Etsy may ask for it anyway.
:::

::: step check | Open the shop
Tap **Open your shop**. Then fill in your **policies** (Settings → Policies, use Etsy's template), your **About** section and a shop icon. A finished shop looks trustworthy, and Etsy says the policy template helps in search.
:::

## Publish your listing, field by field

Go to **Shop Manager → Listings → Add a listing**. Fill it in from top to bottom:

| Field | What to put there |
|---|---|
| **Photos** | Your 5 photos from chapter 7. The hero shot first. Up to 10 are allowed. |
| **Video** | Optional, 5–15 seconds. Your product in use, no sound needed. |
| **Title** | Your title from the template above. |
| **About this listing** | "I did" + "A finished product" + "Made to order" for prints you make after the order. |
| **Category** | Type what it is, pick the closest suggestion. |
| **Attributes** | Fill in every one: color, material, room, occasion. They power Etsy's filters. |
| **Description** | Your description from the template, with the FAQ at the end. |
| **Personalization** | Turn on if you add names. Write clear instructions and a character limit. |
| **Price and quantity** | Your price from the worksheet. Quantity 5–10 for made-to-order items, so it doesn't sell out after one order. |
| **Variations** | Colors or sizes. Give each its own price if they cost more. |
| **Tags** | All 13, from the tag formula below. |
| **Shipping** | Create a shipping profile once (next step) and reuse it. |
| **Return policy** | Pick one. Even "no returns, but I fix problems" beats nothing. |
| **Product safety (EU)** | If you sell to the EU: your name, address, a product code and any warnings (chapter 4). |

::: step truck | Make your shipping profile once
In the listing, under **Shipping**, tap **Create shipping profile**:

- **Ship from:** your postcode
- **Processing time:** how many days you need to make and pack it, for example 1–3 days
- **Where you ship:** your country first, then add the US, UK and the EU when you know the prices
- **Price per country:** from your shipping table in chapter 9. US buyers: under $6, the rest goes in the item price
- **Item weight and size:** with packaging. Etsy uses it for calculated shipping and labels

Save it with a clear name, like `Small flat parcel`. Next time you just pick it.
:::

::: step check | Preview, then publish
Tap **Preview** and look at it on your phone. Is the first photo clear? Does the title say what it is? Then **Publish**. It costs $0.20.
:::

## Your own shop or Instagram?

The same rules apply. Write what it **does**, show the **size**, make **buying easy**. On Instagram, put the price and how to buy in the caption, and the link in your bio.

::: warn | Don't stuff keywords
Titles like "Holder Organizer Stand Storage Box Gift Desk Gamer Switch" look spammy and can hurt you. Write for humans, using buyers' words.
:::

## Selling on eBay or Facebook instead?

The same listing works everywhere, with small changes:

| Platform | What matters most |
|---|---|
| **eBay** | Exact words in the title: brand it fits, model number, part number. Fill in every **item specific**. |
| **Facebook Marketplace** | A clear first photo, the price, and fast replies. Buyers message first, so answer within an hour if you can. |
| **TikTok Shop** | The video sells, the listing confirms. Use the same first photo as your video's best frame. |

## Etsy's newest rules for titles (2026)

Etsy updated its title guidance in April 2026. In short:

::: yesno | Do | Don't
+ Keep titles short: under about **15 words**
+ Name the item clearly, most important traits first (what it is, color, material, size)
+ Put extra details in tags, attributes and the description
- Subjective words like "perfect" or "beautiful"
- Gift phrases and holidays, unless essential to the item
- Repeated words, sale or shipping info
:::

Etsy now looks at **the whole listing**: title, tags, attributes, description, the first photo and even reviews. The title doesn't have to carry every keyword anymore.

**Before:** "Switch Game Holder Organizer Storage Box Gift for Gamer Perfect Christmas Present Desk Stand"
**After:** "Switch Game Holder for 24 Cartridges, Black PLA, Desk Organizer"

## The tag formula

Etsy gives you **13 tags** (each up to 20 characters). Use all 13, as multi-word phrases. Spread them like this:

| Tags | What they describe | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | What it is | switch game holder, cartridge storage |
| 3 | What it fits / who it's for | nintendo switch, gamer gift |
| 2 | Where it's used | desk organizer, game room decor |
| 2 | Style or material | minimalist, 3d printed |
| 2 | Occasions (only if real) | birthday gift, christmas gift |

Phrases you already selected as attributes (like color or occasion) don't need a tag too. Use that space for another search phrase.
