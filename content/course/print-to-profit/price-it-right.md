## Why most sellers charge too little

::: figure price
:::


They look at the filament: "It's only $1 of plastic, I'll sell it for $6." Then they forget:

::: icons
clock | Their time | Removing supports, packing, messages. Often 15–30 min per order.
x | Failed prints | Every few prints, one fails. Someone pays for it. You.
box | Packaging | Box, padding, label.
percent | Fees | Marketplaces and payments take 5–15%.
:::

Selling cheap doesn't get you more sales. It gets you **more work for nothing**, and it makes buyers think your product is cheap.

::: step chart | Add it up and multiply
**Total cost** = material + power + machine + time + packaging

**Price** = total cost × **2.5** (use 2 for simple items, 3+ for personalized or unique ones)

Why multiply? Your profit pays for things you don't see per order: designs that didn't sell, test prints, photos, your printer upgrade, and your taxes.
:::

::: step percent | Add the fees
Marketplaces take a cut of the price. To keep your profit, add the fees on top:

`final price = (price + fixed fee) ÷ (1 − fee %)`

**Example:** price $20, fees 9.5% + $0.45 → ($20 + $0.45) ÷ 0.905 = **$22.60**

The [calculator](/tools/3d-print-price-calculator) does this for you, with Etsy-like fees pre-filled.
:::

## Check it against the market

Now compare with similar listings:

| Your price vs. the market | What to do |
|---|---|
| About the same | 👍 Good. Win on photos and fit. |
| Higher | Fine if yours is better or more specific. Show why in photos. |
| Much higher | Lower your costs (faster print, less support, less packing time) |
| Much lower | **Raise your price.** You're leaving money on the table. |

::: warn | Never compete on being cheapest
Someone will always be cheaper. Compete on **fit, quality, photos and personalization** instead.
:::

## 4 ways to earn more per sale

::: icons
list | Sell sets | A set of 4 costs you almost the same time as 1.
heart | Personalize | A name or color choice can add 30–100% to the price.
zap | Speed up printing | Fewer supports, thicker layers where it doesn't show.
star | Offer a premium version | A "pro" color or material at a higher price.
:::

## How to handle shipping in your price

Two simple options:

- **Free shipping, built into the price.** Buyers love it. Add your average shipping cost to the price.
- **Shipping charged separately.** Fine for local or heavy items.

Whichever you choose, check real postage rates for your country **before** you publish the price. More in chapter 7.
