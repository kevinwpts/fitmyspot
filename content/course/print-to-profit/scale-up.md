::: answer
Scale only **after** you have a product that sells steadily. Then grow in this order: **1)** save time (batch printing, templates), **2)** raise the value per order (sets, personalization, prices), **3)** add products for the same buyers, **4)** add printers, **5)** add business customers. Most people do it in reverse, and lose money.
:::

## Are you ready to scale?

::: yesno | Ready | Not yet
+ You've made at least 10 sales of one product
+ Orders keep coming without you pushing every day
+ You know your profit per order
+ You're running out of printer time
- Sales come only when you post
- You don't know your profit per order
- Your printer is mostly idle
- You want to scale "to make it work"
:::

If you're not ready, go back to chapter 8. More printers don't fix a product nobody buys.

## Grow in 5 steps

::: step clock | Save time first
Your time is the limit long before your printer is.

- **Batch print:** fill the whole plate with the same product
- **Print stock** of your bestseller in popular colors, ready to ship
- **Templates:** for personalization, titles and messages
- **Packing station** (chapter 7) so each order takes 3 minutes
- **Fewer supports:** redesign to print faster with less cleanup
:::

::: step coins | Raise the value per order
It's easier to sell more to the same buyer than to find a new one.

- Sell **sets** and **bundles**
- Add **personalization** as a paid option
- Offer a **premium version** (special color, bigger size)
- **Raise prices** a little when you get busy. If sales don't drop, raise again.
:::

::: step list | Add products for the same buyers
Your buyers already trust you. Ask: **what else do they need?**

Board game inserts → token trays, card holders, insert for the expansion. Desk organizer → cable tray, headphone stand, monitor riser. Each new product makes your shop stronger.
:::

::: step printer | Add printers (carefully)
Add a printer when your **current printer is busy most of the day** with paid orders.

| Before buying | Why |
|---|---|
| Same model as the one you have | Same settings, same spare parts |
| Calculate the payback | Profit per order × extra orders per month |
| Keep a spare-parts box | Nozzles, plates, belts: one broken part shouldn't stop orders |

Two reliable printers beat five unreliable ones.
:::

::: step store | Add business customers
Businesses order more, and they come back. Ideas:

- **Cafés and shops:** signs, menu holders, displays
- **Small brands:** custom stands, packaging inserts, promo items
- **Workshops and makers:** jigs, fixtures, organizers
- **Event planners:** name tags, table numbers, decorations

Bring a sample, show a price list, promise a delivery date you can keep. Ask for 50% upfront on big orders.
:::

## Know your numbers every month

A simple monthly check:

| Number | Why |
|---|---|
| Revenue | Is it growing? |
| Profit per order | Are you still earning on each sale? |
| Best-selling product | Make more of what works |
| Best channel | Where your buyers come from |
| Hours worked | Your real hourly rate = profit ÷ hours |

::: warn | The scaling trap
Buying printers, filament stock and tools before sales justify it is the fastest way to turn a side income into a debt. Grow from profit, not from hope.
:::
