*Last updated: November 19, 2026*

We collect as little as possible. Here is everything.

## Email list

If you sign up for the checklist or a waitlist, we store **your email address**, where you signed up and when. We use it only to send you boredprinter guides and news. Every email has an unsubscribe option, or contact us to be removed.

## Purchases

Payments are handled by **Stripe**, which acts as the seller of record and handles VAT and sales tax. We never see or store your card details. We receive your email and what you bought, so you can download your guides.

## Logging in to My guides

When you request a login link, your email is stored by our login provider (Supabase, EU servers) so we can send you the link and check what you've bought.

## Browser storage

The price calculator remembers your numbers in your own browser (local storage). It never leaves your device.

## Cookies and tracking

We don't use advertising cookies. Our hosting provider (Vercel) may keep anonymous, aggregated visit statistics.

## Your rights

You can ask what we store about you, and ask us to delete it. Contact us via a DM on Instagram: [@boredprinter](https://www.instagram.com/boredprinter/).

## Services we use

- Vercel (hosting)
- Supabase (email list and login)
- Stripe (payments)
- Google Fonts (fonts)
